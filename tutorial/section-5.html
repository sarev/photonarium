Faces — Naming the people in your photos

← Back to contents

5.1. Opening Faces

Click Faces in the toolbar. This is where you name the people Imaginary has found in your photos.

5.2. Faces toolbar

The Faces toolbar has its own controls.

─removeSmaller face thumbnails
✚addLarger face thumbnails

5.3. The Faces screen (unknown faces only)

When you first arrive, you see all the faces Imaginary has detected. None have names yet — that’s your job. Each face has a name field below it.

5.4. Typing a name

Click on a face and type a name into the field below it.

5.5. Naming your first face

Press Enter to save. This creates your first person. A “Known People” section appears at the top of the screen.

5.6. Naming with autocomplete

As you name more faces, suggestions appear while you type. Pick a suggestion to assign this face to an existing person.

5.7. Removing failed face detections

Sometimes Imaginary detects something that isn’t really a face. If you spot one, click it to select it.

5.8. Adding more non-faces to the selection

Right-click to add more items to your selection without losing what you’ve already picked. Here we’ve selected eight false detections.

5.9. Removing the batch

Hover over any selected card and click the red X. Because multiple cards are selected, Imaginary offers to remove them all at once.

5.10. The ignore control

For faces of people you don’t need to name, select them and click the grey minus button. This moves them out of the way without deleting the detection.

5.11. Ignoring faces

Click the grey minus to ignore the selected faces. You can always rename them later if needed.

5.12. Ignored faces result

The ignored faces now belong to a special “–” person in the Known People section. They’re out of the way but not deleted, so you can always find them again.

5.13. Quick Match

Click the sparkle button to see who this face might be. Imaginary shows the closest matches from your named people. With more people named, this becomes a fast way to identify faces — just click a match to assign.

5.14. Naming another face

We didn’t find a quick match so let’s name this face ourselves.

5.15. Known faces list expands

Press Enter to save. Now we have two named people in our collection, plus a group for all of the 'ignored' faces.

5.16. Drag and drop

You can also drag faces directly onto a person’s name to assign them, including into the 'ignore' group. This also works with multiple faces selected.

5.17. Viewing all faces for a person

Double-click a person to see all the faces assigned to them in one place. Here you can seelect (star) a preferred photo (their avatar), and lock correct identifications, and remove any faces with the wrong name.

5.18. Setting a preferred face

Click the star on your favourite image of this person. This becomes their avatar throughout Imaginary.

5.19. Locking and unlocking faces

Locked faces (green padlock) are ones you’ve confirmed. Imaginary uses these as reliable examples when trying to recognise this person in other photos. You cannot unlock the preferred face!

5.20. Returning to the Faces screen

Click the ⊕ / center_focus_strong (Focus on one person) toolbar button, or press Escape, to return to the main Faces screen.
