Duplicates — Cleaning up your collection

← Back to contents

4.1. Opening Duplicates

Click Duplicates in the toolbar to find photos that look the same or very similar. Each “stack” is a group of photos that Imaginary thinks are duplicates. The number shows how many are in each group.

4.2. Duplicates toolbar

The duplicates screen has its own toolbar controls.

4.3. Adjusting strictness

Move the slider to change how strict the matching is. “Identical” finds exact copies. “Related” casts a wider net for photos that are similar but not the same.

4.4. Opening a duplicate group

Double-click a stack to open that group in the Gallery, where you can compare the photos and decide which ones to keep. The one selected is highest resolution with sharpest focus.

4.5. Reviewing duplicates in Gallery

Now you can see the duplicates together. Select the ones you don’t want — you could press Delete to remove them, or just move on to the next group.

4.6. Moving between groups

Use the toolbar arrow buttons (or Alt+Left / Alt+Right) to move to the next or previous duplicate group without going back to the Duplicates screen.
